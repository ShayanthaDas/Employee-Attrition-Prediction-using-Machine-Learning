# Employee Attrition Prediction – Case Study

## 1. Background

Employee attrition is a common issue in organizations. Losing employees regularly increases cost and affects productivity.

This project tries to understand why employees leave using data and machine learning.

---

## 2. Problem

The main questions were:

* Which employees are more likely to leave?
* What factors affect attrition the most?
* Can we predict employee turnover using data?

The goal is to help HR teams make better retention decisions.

---

## 3. Dataset

The dataset includes employee-related information such as:

* Age
* Department
* Job role
* Monthly income
* Job satisfaction
* Work overtime
* Years at company
* Attrition status (target variable)

Some preprocessing was required before analysis.

---

## 4. Approach

### Data cleaning

* Handled missing values
* Converted categorical variables
* Encoded target variable

### Exploratory analysis

Focused on:

* Attrition rate by department
* Salary vs attrition
* Job satisfaction impact
* Overtime effect

### Feature selection

Identified important variables affecting attrition.

---

## 5. Model building

Tested basic classification models:

* Logistic Regression
* Decision Tree
* Random Forest

Evaluated using accuracy and basic performance metrics.

---

## 6. Findings

### OverTime is a major factor

Employees who work overtime regularly are more likely to leave.

---

### Job satisfaction matters

Lower satisfaction levels strongly increase attrition risk.

---

### Salary has impact

Employees with lower income tend to leave more often.

---

### Department differences exist

Some departments show higher attrition compared to others.

---

## 7. Business insights

Attrition is not random. It is influenced by workload, satisfaction, and compensation.

Companies can reduce turnover by focusing on these areas instead of reacting after employees leave.

---

## 8. Recommendations

* Reduce excessive overtime workload
* Improve employee satisfaction monitoring
* Review salary structure for low-income groups
* Focus on high-risk departments

---

## 9. Conclusion

This project shows how machine learning can be used in HR analytics to understand employee behavior and reduce turnover.

Even a simple model can provide useful direction for HR decision-making.

---

* How to connect data insights with business decisions
